My Favorite Series: Jujutsu Kaisen

Jujutsu Kaisen is a Japanese dark fantasy anime series about *Yuji Itadori*, a high school student who joins a secret organization of Jujutsu Sorcerers.

Why I Love It
* Amazing fight choreography and animation
* Well-written characters like **Satoru Gojo** and **Megumi Fushiguro**
* Great soundtrack and intense storyline

Top 3 Favorite Arcs
1. Shibuya Incident Arc
2. Hidden Inventory / Premature Death Arc
3. Kyoto Goodwill Event Arc

"Throughout Heaven and Earth, I alone am the honored one."
